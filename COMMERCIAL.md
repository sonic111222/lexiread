that license, noncommercial use is permitted. Commercial use is not permitted

## What this means in practice

- Personal study, private experimentation, hobby projects and other
  noncommercial uses are permitted under the license.
- Educational institutions, charities, public research organizations, public
  safety/health organizations, environmental organizations and government
  institutions are treated as permitted noncommercial organizations by the
  license.
- Selling the software, selling access to a hosted version, bundling it into a
  paid product, or using it primarily for commercial advantage or monetary
  compensation requires a separate license.
- The project name and logo are not licensed for commercial use and are not
  granted by the software license. Forks should use their own branding.

This summary is for orientation only. The controlling text is the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) and the
[NOTICE](NOTICE). If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, obtain
legal advice or contact the maintainer before using the project commercially.
